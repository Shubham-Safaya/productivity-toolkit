The name travels further than the flower

Much of what is sold as Kashmiri saffron never grew in Kashmir.

Blends, dyed lookalikes, and foreign pass-offs trade freely on the Valley's name.

The Geographical Indication tag exists to end that. We built Kong Posh to honor it.

Our proof, not our word

GI certification

Kashmir saffron carries a registered Geographical Indication. Our lots are sourced within the notified region and documented against it.

Lab-graded, lot by lot

Every lot is tested by an independent laboratory to ISO 3632 — the international saffron standard — for crocin, picrocrocin, and safranal. We publish the grade with the batch.

QR batch verification

Each tin carries a QR code that resolves to its batch record: harvest window, village, grade, and lab date. Open a sample batch record.

Origin

We are a Kashmiri Pandit family venture with roots in Jammu and a long memory of the Valley. Saffron is not a product category to us; it is a homecoming.

We source directly from growers on the Pampore karewas, pay for quality at the farm gate, and put our family name behind every batch we seal.
